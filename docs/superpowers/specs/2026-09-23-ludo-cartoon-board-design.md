# The Ludo board, drawn like a childhood board game - design

## What the owner asked for

"It can be cartoonish, right? Because it is just ludo - it should feel like childhood." The same
owner judged a satin studio render too serious and chose the vector board over it, so the direction
is the printed cardboard board a family keeps in a cupboard, drawn the way a good mobile game draws
it: bright, round, outlined, friendly - and still crisp and tidy enough for an adult.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Vector, generated by `tools/art/boards.mjs`** from the rules' geometry, as today. | A cartoon is flat colour and line - exactly what SVG is good at - and the generator is what keeps every star and arrow on the square the rules put it. |
| C2 | **One ink line on everything**: `#2A1E5C`, a deep indigo, at a weight that reads on a 21 px phone square (board units: 2.6 on tiles, 4 on yards and the tray, 6 on the frame, in a 1024 drawing). | A black outline goes muddy on red and blue; an indigo line is the "cartoon ink" of every sticker sheet and sits well on all four colours and on cream. |
| C3 | **Sticker shading, not gradients**: every filled shape gets a lighter band across its top and a darker lip along its bottom, both inside the outline. | This is what makes a flat shape read as a raised, friendly object; a gradient reads as a web button. |
| C4 | **Paper and a toy frame**: a cream field (`#FFF4DC`) inside a rounded frame in warm toy orange-wood (`#D9843F`, light band `#F0A763`, dark lip `#A85A22`). | Cream is the colour of printed card; the frame is the edge of a toy, and it still sits on the walnut table like the Hokm felt does. |
| C5 | **Brighter board colours, same identity**: red `#FF5A4E`, green `#2FC262`, yellow `#FFC72C`, blue `#3F8CFF` for the painted areas, while the pawns, pips and badges keep `--ludo-*`. | A printed board is lighter than a plastic piece; the pieces must stay the colours the UI already names. |
| C6 | **Yards are round houses**: a rounded square in the colour with a big cream disc (the nest, `NEST`/`NEST_RADIUS` unchanged), four seats as recessed circles in a pale tint with an inner shadow arc along their top. | An empty seat must read as an empty seat and never as a pawn - the lesson of the bead wells. |
| C7 | **Tiles are rounded stickers** with a 0.18-square corner, the ink line, and a small gap of grout (`#E9D9B8`). Start squares are the colour with a white outlined star; safe squares are cream with a star in the arm's colour; home runs are the colour. | Same rules, friendlier shapes. |
| C8 | **Arrows and stars are chunky**: a rounded five-point star with an ink outline and a white glint; an arrow with a round tail. | Thin geometric stars are the grown-up version. |
| C9 | **The centre is a crown**: four outlined triangles in the colours meeting on a cream medallion carrying a gold star with an ink outline. | The goal of the game should look like one. |
| C10 | **A few glints, no clutter**: one small white glint at the top-left of each yard and on each star. No characters, no clouds, no text on the board. | Playful, not busy - the pieces are the characters. |
| C11 | **The pawns stay the rendered plastic pegs.** | Childhood ludo pieces ARE glossy plastic; the keyline already matches an outlined board. |
| C12 | **Geometry does not move.** `MARGIN`, `CELL`, `NEST`, `NEST_SPREAD`, `HOME_SLOTS` and the frame fraction `RIM` are unchanged, so the renderer, the fallback and the tests are untouched. | The board is a new picture of the same object. |

## What is not changing

The DOM renderer, the plates beside the board, the die, the pawns, the motion and the sounds.

## Verification

- `node tools/art/boards.mjs` writes the board; the ring still has 52 cells or it throws.
- Side by side with the current board at 360 px and 564 px in the visible browser, with pawns placed
  in yards, on the track, on a start square and in the home triangle.
- At 360 px: every tile edge, star and seat is distinct; no seat can be mistaken for a pawn; the ink
  line is not heavier than the pawn's keyline.
- `npm run check`, `npm test`, `npm run build` (the board is a public file, not a budgeted chunk),
  `npm run qa` against the built server, then screenshots of the table at 390 and 1280 in both
  languages.
