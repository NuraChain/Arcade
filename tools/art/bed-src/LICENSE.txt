Bed sources

Every file here is released under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Each is the stretch of the original recording that its bed plays, kept so the bed can be built again,
re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis. A piece of music is cut where its last four seconds sound as its first four
do, because the player hands its loop over during them.

From OpenGameArt (opengameart.org), music whose page lists CC0 among its licences:

- opengameart-bedazzled.ogg, "Bedazzled" by Kistol, played behind Ludo,
  from 0.302 to 56.076 seconds of it:
  https://opengameart.org/content/bedazzled
- opengameart-town-3.ogg, "Town 3" by Pro Sensory, played behind Hokm,
  from 31.509 to 93.112 seconds of it:
  https://opengameart.org/content/town-3
- opengameart-medieval-the-bards-tale.ogg, "Medieval: The Bard's Tale" by RandomMind, played behind
  backgammon, the whole of its loop version with its first 4 seconds again at the end:
  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-bards-tale
- opengameart-just-you-and-me-guitar.ogg, "Just you and me Guitar" by Pro Sensory, played behind poker,
